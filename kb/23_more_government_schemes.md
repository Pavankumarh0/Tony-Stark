# More Government Schemes for Farmers

Review status: scheme rules and amounts change often. Confirm details at the Raitha Samparka Kendra, the bank, or the official scheme website before applying. Smart Krishi does not state subsidy amounts.

## Kisan Credit Card (KCC)
The Kisan Credit Card gives farmers short term loans for crop cultivation and related needs from banks at a concessional interest rate, with a further reduction for repaying on time. It also covers animal husbandry and fisheries farmers. Apply at your bank branch with land records and identity proof.

## e-NAM online market
The National Agriculture Market (e-NAM) is an online trading platform that links mandis, so farmers can sell their produce to buyers in other markets and get payment online. Register through the local APMC market or the e-NAM website.

## PM-KUSUM solar pumps
Under PM-KUSUM, farmers can get subsidy to install solar powered irrigation pumps and to solarise existing pumps, which reduces the cost of diesel and electricity. Apply through the state renewable energy or agriculture department.

## PMKSY: Per Drop More Crop
The Pradhan Mantri Krishi Sinchayee Yojana supports micro irrigation, that is drip and sprinkler systems, under its Per Drop More Crop component, with higher subsidy for small and marginal farmers. Apply through the agriculture or horticulture department.

## Krishi Bhagya (Karnataka)
Krishi Bhagya is a Karnataka scheme for rainfed areas that supports farm ponds to collect rain water, with lining, diesel or solar pumps and micro irrigation for protective irrigation. Apply at the Raitha Samparka Kendra.

## Farmer Producer Organisations (FPOs)
The government supports the formation of Farmer Producer Organisations, where farmers join together to buy inputs, store, process and sell produce at better prices. Ask the agriculture department, NABARD or the KVK about FPOs in your area.

## Agriculture Infrastructure Fund
The Agriculture Infrastructure Fund gives loans with interest subvention for post-harvest infrastructure such as warehouses, cold storage, sorting and grading units and processing units, for farmers, FPOs and cooperatives.

## Animal husbandry support
Schemes support dairy, sheep and goat rearing, poultry and fisheries, including livestock insurance and vaccination. Contact the veterinary hospital or the animal husbandry department.
