# Farming by the Weather

Review status: general guide compiled for Smart Krishi from standard agrometeorology advice. Smart Krishi gives the local weather forecast and rain alert from OpenWeatherMap; the India Meteorological Department (IMD) district agromet advisories and the Meghdoot app give official forecasts with crop advice.

## Before heavy rain
When heavy rain is forecast, do not spray pesticides or apply fertilizer, because the rain will wash them away. Harvest crops that are ready and move harvested produce to a covered place. Open drainage channels so water does not stand in the field. Stake tall crops such as banana and sugarcane.

## During a dry spell
When rain is delayed or a dry spell continues, give protective irrigation at the critical stage of the crop if water is available. Mulch the soil to hold moisture. Remove weeds, which compete for water. Do not apply top dressing of fertilizer on dry soil. If sowing is greatly delayed, choose a short duration variety or a contingency crop such as horse gram, which the agriculture department suggests for late sowing.

## In very hot weather
In very hot weather, irrigate in the evening or early morning, mulch the soil, and give shade to nurseries and young plants. Spraying water on vegetables in the evening reduces heat stress. Give livestock shade, plenty of clean drinking water and feed in the cooler hours.

## In cold weather
In cold weather, light irrigation in the evening protects crops from frost in the cold areas. Cover nurseries at night. Cold, wet weather increases fungal diseases such as late blight in potato and tomato, so watch the crop closely.

## Humid and cloudy weather
High humidity and cloudy, wet weather favour fungal diseases such as blast in rice and ragi, late blight, downy mildew and fruit rot. Keep enough space between plants for air to move, avoid excess nitrogen fertilizer, remove infected leaves, and ask the agriculture officer for protective measures when such weather continues.

## Wind
Do not spray in strong wind, because the spray drifts away from the crop and can harm people and nearby crops. Stake or prop banana, sugarcane and tall vegetables before strong winds.

## Spraying safely
Spray in the early morning or evening, when it is cool and calm, and not before rain. Wear a mask, gloves and full clothing while spraying, do not eat, drink or smoke while spraying, and wash well afterwards. Keep pesticides locked away from children, food and animals, and never reuse empty pesticide containers. Pregnant women, children and the elderly should not handle pesticides.
